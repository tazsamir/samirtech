About me

Hello, I’m Tahir⌗

I’m a Senior Technical Support Engineer based in the West Midlands, UK. I troubleshoot operating systems, networks, databases, integrations and enterprise software, and I enjoy turning difficult problems into clear documentation.

Experience⌗

Snow Software · Technical Support Engineer⌗

SOTI · Technical Analyst / Senior Technical Analyst⌗

Technology and homelab⌗

I have been building and repairing computers since the Windows 3.1 era and have used Linux as my main desktop platform since 2022. My current setup includes Fedora with KDE, a Docker host, two TrueNAS systems, Proxmox experiments and encrypted off-site backups.

I document the homelab here because reliable infrastructure is less about collecting hardware and more about understanding failure, recovery and maintenance.

Privacy and Linux⌗

I’m interested in practical privacy, open-source software and reducing unnecessary dependence on cloud services. I currently use Fedora, Docker and self-hosted services while keeping convenience and recoverability in balance.
